## Who am I?
I am Nico, a data scientist with over five years of experience in machine learning and AI, including generative models and retrieval systems. I hold a bachelor’s degree in Statistics and a master’s degree in Computer Science focused on data quality.

My work focuses on the end-to-end lifecycle of a model, from how data is produced and governed to system architecture and real-world utility. To build and scale these solutions, I rely primarily on Python, SQL, and Databricks.

Away from data and code, you’ll likely find me playing chess, practicing the violin, or spending a weekend cooking. I also enjoy going to the theater and unwinding with PC games.

## What is NicoDataHub?
This is my personal digital space to write, experiment, and share practical lessons on building reliable data and AI systems. To keep things structured for both reading and quick reference, the site focuses on two areas:
- **Articles & Notes**: Deep dives and thoughts on the technical challenges I encounter, visualize, or form opinions on.
- **Resources & Tools**: Easy-to-access templates, prompt frameworks, and cheat sheets designed to streamline workflows for both myself and fellow developers.

The goal is simply to keep practical lessons and reusable solutions documented in one place. As I explore new problems and refine my workflows, this space continues to evolve with them.
